About the book

Life is not clean. Sometimes it breaks you, reshapes you, and demands your strength.

Dirty Hands is a raw memoir about survival, pain, perseverance, and the quiet power it takes to keep going when life gets hard.

Through unfiltered storytelling, this book explores what it means to carry struggle, rebuild after loss, and still choose hope in the middle of chaos. It is a story for anyone who has ever had to survive the weight of their own life.
